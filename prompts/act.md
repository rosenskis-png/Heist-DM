# Act call

An act is beginning. You decide what it holds: what its region is like now, what it's busy with, who holds it, and who or what stands at its climax. You're deciding from what the party has actually done, so the act answers their choices. You don't design any room: rooms are written later, one at a time, from what you decide here.

This is pulp fantasy played straight: bold, visible and a little larger than life.

## What you write
- **goal:** the act's job, given to you, said in this world's own terms and this party's situation.
- **region:** what the region is like now, in a few sentences: how it looks, sounds and moves, who's there and what they're doing, and what's dangerous about it. If earlier acts changed things (a faction humbled, an alarm raised, a war started), it shows here.
- **holders:** the ids of the factions that hold the region now. Start from the region's holders, and change them if what happened would.
- **busy:** what the region is busy with, one short line for each kind that really happens here, in this world's terms. At least four kinds, and as many as fit. Leave out kinds that wouldn't happen here.
- **peak:** who or what stands at the act's climax.
  - **who:** one of the factions' leaders, from the rung that fits this act, or something that isn't a person when the act calls for it. In Act 4 it may be several designs coming to a head at once.
  - **why:** whose design blocks the party's way, and so why it's them. Choose by who is in the way, not by who the party likes: someone the party helped before can still be in the way now.
  - **wants:** what they want right now.
  - **turns:** how they act as the climax goes on, the way a boss fight has turns: what they do first, what they do when pressed, what they do when losing.
  - **where:** where in the region the climax happens.
- **hints:** two or three visible signs of the peak that rooms can show before the climax, each on its own: something a party would see or hear, never the peak in person.

## How the acts rise
Tension, danger and grandeur climb from Act 1 to Act 4: the peak climbs the ladders, from a local face early to the top, or the factions' grandest designs, at the heart. Act 5 is the escape and the consequences: what the party did comes back at them. Never choose a peak who has already been one. After Act 5, an act follows what the player says they want to do.
