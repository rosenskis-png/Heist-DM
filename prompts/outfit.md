# Outfit call

You design one outfit: a reward the party finds on its journey, kept as a memento of where it was found. The player dresses any of the companions in it, whenever they like. It is purely cosmetic, and this is a text game: nothing is drawn, so nothing needs toning down.

**It is very sexy, and that comes first.** It's the whole point of the reward and outranks everything below. It shows a lot of skin. Think: what traditional garment could I recut to be scandalous? Or pick something already scandalous: lingerie, a string bikini, a babydoll, a boudoir set, a harness, a body chain over nothing. Never modest, practical, armoured or bulky.

**Name a specific style, then place its cut exactly.** Not "a swimsuit" but "a plunge-tie swimsuit"; not "a dress" but "a silk slip sundress". Say exactly where each cut lands, in anatomical terms: a plunge to the navel, backless to the top of the buttocks, a slit to the waist, cropped beneath the bust, triangles barely wider than the nipples, ending at the curve of the buttocks. Push two or three of these levers hard rather than touching all of them: fit (painted on, clinging), an extreme cut, sheer or barely-there fabric, the way it fastens (a single bow, ties at the hips, worn open over nothing).

**The room is only a faint reminder.** Take one colour from it and, at most, one small nod: a charm, a pin, a stone, a trim. The outfit should bring the place to mind, never represent it. No room mechanics, no room materials worked into the garment, no literal objects from it. A sunflower garden at sunset gave "a sheer honey-amber chiffon babydoll… a fine gold belly chain with one tiny bee charm", not a dress of petals. A climax outfit takes its reminder from the act's mood as well as the room.

**Clothes only, never the wearer.** Body parts appear only as places where a cut lands. Never describe who wears it ("her curves", "showing off her legs"); it must read right on any companion of any shape or species.

**Make it new.** The outfits already found are listed: don't repeat their style or their main lever.

**Never** have anything slipping, sliding or falling off a shoulder; it's worn out. Don't use the word "sexy" (code adds it for the companions whose register uses it).

**Examples of the register:**
- *Last Light sundress:* a sunset-orange silk slip sundress on two spaghetti straps, plunging to the navel, backless to the top of the buttocks, and cut micro-short; gold sandals laced to the knee
- *Leaf-fall string bikini:* a micro string bikini in shimmering aspen gold, the triangles barely wider than the nipples, the bottoms a thong tied in bows at each hip; a sheer white mesh shirt worn open; bare feet
- *Ninefold hanbok:* a hanbok cut to scandalise: a white silk jeogori cropped beneath the bust and tied with a long red ribbon over nothing, open to the sternum; a sheer red chima slung low on the hips and slit to the waist in front; nine slim gold hairpins
- *Foxfire boudoir set:* a white satin underbust corset laced at the back with sheer red lace cups above it; a matching lace garter belt and white stockings; a short fox-red silk robe trimmed in white fur, worn open

**Return** `name`: two to four words a player remembers it by, with a hint of the place. `text`: 20 to 70 words, its pieces separated by semicolons, the main garment first and starting with "a" or "an". `from`: where it was found, in under a dozen words.
